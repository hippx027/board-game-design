## Design issues (for the designer)
1. **Medic Heal loop (near game-breaking in 2p, thought test):** holding one Heal card and recycling it every turn clears 3+ Dead cards per use
2. **Mechanic upgrades almost every turn:** cost 3 matches the starting 3 Move 1 / 3 Heal 1 exactly, and paid cards recycle
3. **Mechanic buys Base Shield cheaper than the Tank's whole ability**
4. **Teach load:** suggested learning-game cuts: supply drops, loot piles, upgrades, the loot display, the "critical" call

## Wording fixes applied
Movement defined (adjacent steps, no crossing gaps, shared tiles) · kept cards named · discard to 3 lets you keep fewer · upgrade cost wording follows character changes; can't upgrade a stat already at 4 · storm marker choice may cut off opponents · drop placer rotation and nudge timing · Medic heals value +2 and the card goes to discard with played cards · Tank: all upgrades (any stat) cost 5
