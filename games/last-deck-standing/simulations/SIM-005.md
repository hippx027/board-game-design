# SIM-005 — Bundle cap, why fight, Heal from the discard pile

Date 2026-09-29 · current rules (pre-made loot tiles, 18-card supplies, gold drops, 5/3/1 loot piles) · scripts `fight_test.py`, `heal_test.py`

## Bundle cap (5p, one bot among a mixed field, fair share 20%)
| Cap | Aggressive | Balanced | Cautious | Median rounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| None | 12% | 18% | 23% | 20 |
| 3 | 12% | 18% | 24% | 20 |
| 2 | 12% | 19% | 28% | 19 |

A cap of 3 has no system cost. A cap of 2 helps cautious play.

## Why fight
2-player duels (row player's win rate):

| | vs Brawler | vs Skirmisher | vs Cautious |
|---|---|---|---|
| Brawler (all in, stays) | — | 49% | 73% |
| Skirmisher (hit and run) | 44% | — | 71% |
| Cautious | 27% | 28% | — |

5 players, mixed field: brawler 8%, skirmisher 19%, cautious 27%.

**Fighting wins one-on-one; it loses in a crowd.** Staying in range after going all in costs a lot when third parties are around (8% vs 19%). This is the battle royale shape: hang back early, fight late.

## Heal from the discard pile
| | 3p median rounds | 5p median rounds | Dead healed per 5p game | Wasted Heals per 5p game | 5p cautious wins |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Hand only | 13 | 18 | 7.9 | 8.0 | 43% |
| Hand or discard | 14 | 19 | 11.9 | 7.2 | 46% |

Heal from the discard pile heals about 50% more Dead cards and wastes about 10% fewer Heals, for about 1 extra round. It slightly favours cautious play.
