## Results
**Reverse pawn placement fixes the turn-order imbalance. Push-off is no longer needed to prevent stalls. 2 tiles per turn barely shortens 8–10 player games. Dropping value-3 cards has no system benefit.**

| Reverse placement order | 18–20% / 18% | 117 | 0% |

## Open
- Length at 8–10 players is driven by total turns (players × rounds), not the storm. Teams or simultaneous turns are the likely fix.
- Supply decks run out in about 70% of 5-player games and in nearly all 8–10 player games.
