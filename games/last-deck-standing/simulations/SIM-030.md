# SIM-030 — Realtime v2 first pass (`sim/lds_rt.py`)

Rules: `rulebook-v2-draft.md`. 300 games per cell, seed 42, mixed bots (aggressive / balanced / cautious by seat), per-player speed 0.7–1.3×, turn ≈ 20 s + 5 s per action (≈ 40 s). Times are wall-clock minutes (p10 / median / p90), pauses included. System evidence only: bots can't say whether realtime is fun.

## Headline: games are far too short
As drafted (elimination at 3 Dead), the median game takes **7–11 min** (3–5 players), against a 30–60 min target. Realtime plays up to N turns at once, so the same ~28–48 total turns that took 30+ min in v1 take about 1/N of the time. The storm reaches only 1.6–2.0 rings and about 2.7 drops arrive before the game ends, so neither timer shapes the game yet.

## Attack options (3p / 5p median min; whiffs/dodges negligible)
| Option | 3p | 5p | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| none (instant) | 7.1 | 8.2 | baseline, 12% of wall time is storm/drop pauses |
| A pause 8 s | 8.3 | 10.9 | +1.2 / +2.7 min; balance identical |
| B dodge with a Move card | 7.2 | 8.1 | about 1 attack in 20 is dodged; no balance change |
| C resolves after 5 s | 7.1 | 8.2 | no balance change |

None of the options changes who wins. A is the only one that costs time (pauses are 27–34% of the game). Pick on feel at the table; B and C need human testing for whether the response window creates arguments.

## Other levers (3p / 5p median min)
- **Base Move/Attack 1** (free): 8.5 / 11.7. Many more attacks (17 / 31 vs 11 / 21), and aggressive bots do worse (11–18%).
- **Loot 1s variant:** 8.3 / 10.9, no change.
- **Cube refresh +6 per drop:** 8.4 / 11.6, +30% loot, no change in length.
- **Elimination at 4 Dead:** 10.0 / 12.8, 1–2% stalls. **At 5:** 14 / 16.5 but **19% / 41% stalls**. The storm doesn't force an ending because storm Dead cards land in the discard pile and get healed.
- **Storm interval** (90 / 150 / 240 s, with elimination at 4): changes length by about ±1 min. It's irrelevant until games run long enough for rings to close.
- **Teams (4p, 2v2):** rotating members (A) 13.0 min; all members at once (C) 8.7 min. Rotation is slower because a team is one stream.

## Balance
- Speed doesn't help: fastest player wins 17–31%, slowest 20–39% (fair is 20–33%). Bots get no benefit from extra turns, which may be a bot limit; check with humans whether speed matters.
- Aggressive bots win the least; cautious/balanced bots do well (1p-per-seat shares are uneven at 5p, so compare with each profile's seat share).

## Caveats
- Turn time is an assumption (40 s). Humans may be faster in realtime.
- Bots don't use Heal-hold, split attacks or the Market strategically beyond greedy buys; eliminated players' cards leave the game (v2 draft doesn't say).
- Gold deck of 12 empties after ~3 drops at 5p.
- The first run used a ragged board where almost every tile was Ring 1; the board is now built compact. The numbers above are after the fix.

## Decisions for the designer
1. Game length: how to add 3–4× more turns (options: elimination at 4 plus fixing the storm so it forces an end; more Dead cards needed to knock out; slower attack, e.g. attacks cost more).
2. Attack option (A/B/C) and whether to pay the time for the pause.
3. Base Move/Attack: none or free 1.

## Addendum: turn speed and a turn floor
Measured turn length in the first runs: mean 37 s (p10–p90 21–62 s). Rerun with the designer's 10–40 s turns (no attack pause): average 19 s gives 3.9 / 4.6 min (3p / 5p); 11 s gives 2.3 / 2.7; 7 s gives 1.3 / 1.6. Turn count stays 28–61 per game, so faster turns just shorten it, and at those speeds the storm and drops barely start.

**Turn floor** (`--turn-floor`, a turn can't end before N seconds, like a sand timer), with ~11 s natural turns, median min at 3p / 5p:

| Floor | 3p | 5p | Storm rings | Fastest player wins (3p) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 2.3 | 2.7 | 0.3 | 36% |
| 20 s | 4.0 | 4.7 | 0.8–1.0 | 37% |
| 30 s | 5.2 | 6.2 | 1.2–1.5 | 42% |
| 45 s | 7.9 | 9.6 | 2.0–2.4 | 43% |
| 60 s | 10.5 | 12.5 | 2.6–3.1 | 45% |
| 90 s | 15.2 | 16.5 | 3.2–3.5 | 48% |

A floor works as a pace dial: length rises about linearly with it and the storm starts to matter at 45 s and up. Even a 90 s floor reaches only 15–17 min, so the 30–60 min target needs a floor around 3 minutes (impractical) or fewer, longer fights. With a long floor the fastest player starts winning more (48% vs 23% for the slowest at 3p), so a floor also fixes the "speed doesn't help" result and may feel more like a race.

## Addendum 2: range costs 2 per tile (designer change)
`--range-cost 2` (now the sim default; v1 was 1). Median min at 3p / 5p:

| Setup | Range 1 | Range 2 |
|---|---|---|
| ~11 s turns, no floor | 2.3 / 2.7 | 3.5 / 3.8 |
| ~11 s turns, 45 s floor | 7.9 / 9.6 | 8.6 / 10.6 |
| ~37 s turns | 7.1 / 8.2 | 7.9 / 8.9 |

Attacks per game fall (3p: 11 to 8.4–8.9; 5p: 20.6 to 16.4) and games run 10–50% longer, with more turns (3p 27.7 to 35.4), so range-2 helps but doesn't close the gap to 30–60 min. Fights are now mostly same-tile or adjacent. With a free Base Attack 1 plus range 2, aggressive bots win only 12% at 3p, so a base attack of 1 is nearly useless at range and mostly helps the cautious style.

## Addendum 3: elimination drop (`--elim-drop`)
Eliminated players leave 0, 2 or 3 gold cubes on their tile. Median min at 3p / 4p / 5p: 9.1 / 10.4 / 12.0 (none), 9.5 / 11.0 / 12.3 (2), 9.5 / 11.0 / 12.1 (3). Loots per game rise about 1.5 and attacks 1; win shares and stalls don't change. Two cubes is enough; three adds nothing. Eliminated players' own cards leave the game.

## Addendum 4: Gold deck 24
Gold deck doubled to 24 cards (4 each of Move 3/4, Attack 3/4, Heal 3/4). No balance change: 3p/5p median 9.0 / 12.7 min, no stalls.

## Addendum 5: fallen player's gold cards loot first
Gold cards are now tagged; an eliminated player's gold cards stay on their tile and are taken before the Gold deck (extra cubes if they had more than 2). Medians unchanged: 9.1 / 11.0 / 13.0 min at 3 / 4 / 5p, no stalls.


## Addendum 6 — looting on entry, storm on entry, 2-minute storm

Changes (designer's calls): loot only when you move into a tile (start tile excluded; gold cubes under you are picked up), storm interval 120 s, and 1 Dead card per storm tile moved into (`--storm-mode enter`; the old end-of-turn charge is `--storm-mode end`). Turn floor: none.

| Storm mode | Players | Turn length | Stalls | Median game (min) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| end | 3 | 37 s | 0.0% | 8.5 |
| end | 5 | 37 s | 0.3% | 12.5 |
| enter | 3 | 37 s | 0.0% | 8.9 |
| enter | 5 | 37 s | 3.7% | 13.1 |
| end | 3 / 5 | 19 s | 0.0% / 0.3% | 5.7 / 8.1 |
| enter | 3 / 5 | 19 s | 0.0% / 0.7% | 5.8 / 8.3 |

Reading: enter-damage plays about the same as end-of-turn damage, with slightly more 5-player stalls at slow turns (3.7%), since a pawn that stops moving takes no damage. Open question for the designer: should standing in a storm cost anything?

**Addendum 6b — both charges (designer's call).** Entering a storm tile costs 1 Dead card, and ending a turn on one costs 1 more (`--storm-mode both`, now the default). Stalls 0.0% (3p) and 1.0% (5p) at both 37 s and 19 s turns; median game 8.5 / 12.3 min at 37 s and 5.6 / 8.2 min at 19 s. The 3.7% stall from enter-only goes away.

## Addendum 7 — starting loot cubes as a lever

`--start-cubes` = share of tiles that start with a cube; everything else at the current rules (both storm charges, loot on entry, 37 s turns, no floor). Median game in minutes:

| Cubes on | 3p | 5p | 5p stalls | Loot per game (3p / 5p) | Market buys (3p / 5p) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20% of tiles | 7.8 | 10.6 | 1.7% | 13.5 / 25.4 | 8.5 / 13.6 |
| 35% | 8.4 | 11.5 | 2.0% | 17.3 / 31.7 | 8.8 / 14.0 |
| 50% | 8.5 | 12.3 | 1.0% | 20.4 / 38.0 | 8.8 / 14.0 |
| 75% | 9.4 | 13.3 | 2.3% | 26.5 / 46.2 | 9.2 / 14.3 |
| 100% | 10.2 | 14.0 | 4.3% | 32.5 / 52.2 | 9.3 / 14.1 |

Reading: it is a weak, roughly linear length dial (about +2.5 min from 20% to 100%). Market buys barely change, so the Market, not loot, is the bottleneck on deck growth. Win rates by play style stay within the noise (cautious 43–52% at 3p). More cubes means more attacks per game (14 to 19.5 at 3p) because players hold stronger hands; 100% adds stalls at 5p. 50% is a fine start; the tile count at your table matters more than the percentage, so set it as "cubes = about half the tiles".

## Addendum 8 — team sizes (5v5, 3v3v3, duos up to 10 players)

100 games per row, 37 s turns, current rules. Mode A = members rotate one at a time per team; mode C = all members play at once.

| Layout | Mode | Stalls | Median game (min) | Storm eliminations | Attack eliminations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2v2 | A / C | 1% / 0% | 14.3 / 9.6 | 2.1 / 1.0 | 0.5 / 1.7 |
| 3v3 | A / C | 10% / 5% | 20.4 / 13.6 | 4.0 / 1.6 | 0.1 / 2.9 |
| 5v5 | A / C | 24% / 4% | 31.6 / 20.6 | 7.2 / 2.3 | 0.1 / 6.0 |
| 3v3v3 | A / C | 19% / 2% | 26.0 / 18.0 | 6.6 / 2.1 | 0.6 / 5.5 |
| 3 duos (6p) | A / C | 8% / 2% | 17.7 / 14.0 | 3.8 / 1.8 | 0.8 / 2.9 |
| 4 duos (8p) | A / C | 13% / 4% | 20.5 / 17.2 | 4.9 / 2.0 | 1.8 / 4.7 |
| 5 duos (10p) | A / C | 16% / 5% | 24.4 / 20.4 | 5.4 / 2.6 | 3.1 / 6.2 |

Reading:
- **Mode A (rotation) does not scale.** A team of 5 gets one turn per 5 of its own turns, so the storm kills teammates who haven't acted yet (7.2 storm eliminations in 5v5, only 0.1 by attack) and 24% of 5v5 games stall. Rotation works at 2v2; past 3 a side it is bad.
- **Mode C (simultaneous) scales fine.** Stalls stay at 2–5% and games run 14–21 minutes at 6–10 players; it plays like the solo game with shared wins.
- Team count matters more than team size for length: 5v5 and 5 duos both end around 20 minutes in mode C.
- Caveat: with 3 teams (3v3v3, 3 duos) my bot seats put a single play style on each team (seat mod 3), so those team-win splits (17/31/52%) measure play style, not seat order. The 2-, 4- and 5-team rows mix styles and are fair. 5v5 mode C also shows a 36/64 split, which is likely noise at 100 runs.

## Addendum 9 — fixes for rotation stalls in big teams

Rotation (`--team-mode A`), 100 games each, 37 s turns. "Stall" = game passed 90 minutes of table time.

| Variant | 3v3 stalls | 5v5 stalls | 3v3v3 stalls | 5v5 median (min) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current (enter + end of turn, 2 min) | 10% | 24% | 19% | 31.6 |
| Storm every 3 min | 10% | 18% | 15% | 34.9 |
| Storm every 4 min | 6% | 14% | 11% | 35.2 |
| Enter charge only | 17% | 43% | 42% | 36.4 |
| End-of-turn charge only | 0% | 1% | 0% | 34.5 |
| Enter only + 3 min | 15% | 39% | 35% | 35.8 |

Reading: a slower storm helps a little, but the enter charge is what causes the stalls; the end-of-turn charge alone has none. Stalled games (`--debug-stall`) all end the same way: the whole board is stormed and the surviving players sit in it holding exactly 2 Dead cards each, with discard piles of 80–370 cards. Likely cause (not verified): the bots avoid moving into storm tiles to dodge the entry charge, so no one approaches to fight and the game idles. That would be a bot-behaviour artifact, and humans may behave differently. Not a rules decision; the designer's rule (enter + end of turn) is unchanged.

## Addendum 10 — hand cap on looting (`--loot-cap`)

Looted cards go into the hand until it reaches the cap; after that they go to the discard pile. Dead cards count toward the cap. 300 games, 37 s turns, current rules.

| Cap | Players | Stalls | Median game (min) | Overflow loot / game | Loot / game | Heals used | Market buys |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none | 3 / 5 | 0.0% / 1.0% | 8.5 / 12.3 | 0 | 20.4 / 38.0 | 5.0 / 10.0 | 8.8 / 14.0 |
| 7 | 3 / 5 | 0.0% / 2.3% | 8.5 / 12.3 | 1.3 / 6.2 | 20.5 / 38.0 | 4.9 / 9.6 | 8.8 / 14.0 |
| **5** | 3 / 5 | 0.3% / 2.7% | 8.4 / 11.5 | 5.4 / 14.9 | 20.5 / 37.4 | 4.6 / 8.8 | 9.0 / 14.1 |
| 4 | 3 / 5 | 0.7% / 4.7% | 8.2 / 11.5 | 8.0 / 19.5 | 20.3 / 37.6 | 4.4 / 8.6 | 8.9 / 14.1 |

Reading: a cap of 5 costs almost nothing. Game length, attacks and Market buys stay put and win rates move within noise (cautious 45–47% at 3p). About a quarter to a third of loot (5.4 of 20.5 at 3p, 14.9 of 37.4 at 5p) overflows to the discard pile, which is the intended price. Heals used drop by about 10%, since fewer loot-to-hand Heals are playable, and 5p stalls rise a little (1.0% to 2.7%). Caps below 5 start to push stalls up (4.7% at 4). Nothing here argues against starting at 5.

## Addendum 12 — weaker loot and cheaper Market costs

`--loot-weak`: loot values 1 and 2 only (half of each deck are 1s, no 3s). `--cheap-market`: every Market cost is one card smaller (Attack 2 = 2 Attack, Move 2 = Move + Heal, Heal 2 = Heal + Attack, Attack 3 = 2 Attack + Move, Move 3 = 2 Move + Heal, Heal 3 = 2 Heal + Attack). 300 games each, 37 s turns, current rules.

| Variant | Players | Stalls | Median game (min) | Market buys | Attacks | Attack eliminations |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current | 3 / 5 | 0.3% / 2.7% | 8.4 / 11.5 | 9.0 / 14.1 | 15.3 / 32.7 | 1.2 / 2.5 |
| Weaker loot | 3 / 5 | 2.3% / 2.3% | 8.7 / 11.8 | 9.3 / 14.1 | 19.3 / 33.5 | 1.2 / 2.4 |
| Cheaper Market | 3 / 5 | 15.3% / 32.3% | 9.3 / 12.2 | 14.3 / 20.1 | 48.9 / 99.6 | 0.9 / 2.1 |
| Both | 3 / 5 | 21.0% / 31.7% | 9.3 / 12.8 | 14.7 / 20.4 | 61.7 / 104.8 | 0.9 / 2.1 |

Reading: weaker loot alone changes nothing important. Cheaper Market costs do raise Market buys by about half (9 to 14 at 3p), which is the point, but they cause many stalls (15% at 3p, 32% at 5p) and a big jump in attacks (49 to 100 per game) with no more attack eliminations. Players end up with strong decks and plenty of Heals, they trade blows, and no one gets eliminated. Cause not diagnosed. So the cheaper costs trade the underused-Market worry for a stalling-game worry; a smaller change (cheaper only the value-2 cards, or one cost per row) may sit in between and has not been tested.

## Addendum 13 — cheaper Market costs on the value-2 cards only

`--cheap-market two`: only the value-2 Market cards get the one-card-cheaper cost (Attack 2 = 2 Attack, Move 2 = Move + Heal, Heal 2 = Heal + Attack); the 3s keep their cost. 300 games, 37 s turns.

| Variant | Players | Stalls | Median game (min) | Market buys | Attacks |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current costs | 3 / 5 | 0.3% / 2.7% | 8.4 / 11.5 | 9.0 / 14.1 | 15.3 / 32.7 |
| Cheaper 2s only | 3 / 5 | 14.7% / 21.7% | 9.1 / 12.4 | 11.3 / 17.3 | 48.5 / 78.5 |
| Cheaper 2s + weaker loot | 3 / 5 | 7.7% / 20.7% | 9.7 / 12.7 | 11.9 / 17.6 | 33.1 / 74.9 |

Reading: even the mild cut raises stalls to 15–22%, so the stall problem isn't about the 3s. Weaker loot softens it a little at 3p (14.7% to 7.7%) but not at 5p. Any cheaper cost seems to tip the sim into the strong-deck trading-blows stalemate seen in addendum 12. Recommendation: keep the current costs for the first session, count real Market buys, and don't change costs on sim evidence alone.

## Addendum 14 — Storm every 4 min (2026-10-12)

Designer asked for the storm at 4 min, then every 4 min. One variable (`--storm-interval`), 300 runs, mixed bots, current rules (drops stay at 2 min, cubes on half the tiles).

| Players | Storm interval | Stalls | Median min (p10–p90) | Storm elims/game | Attack elims/game | Rings marked |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 2 min | 0.3% | 8.4 (5.7–11.9) | 0.8 | 1.2 | 2.3 |
| 3 | 4 min | 2.0% | 8.6 (5.6–13.0) | 0.2 | 1.8 | 1.2 |
| 4 | 2 min | 3.7% | 9.9 (6.9–14.0) | 1.1 | 1.9 | 2.6 |
| 4 | 4 min | 1.3% | 11.0 (7.0–15.6) | 0.4 | 2.6 | 1.4 |
| 6 | 2 min | 8.3% | 13.4 (10.0–17.8) | 1.8 | 3.1 | 3.3 |
| 6 | 4 min | 1.3% | 13.9 (9.9–19.0) | 0.5 | 4.5 | 1.7 |

(3 and 5 min were also run; 3 min sits between, 5 min is the same shape with fewer rings.)

- Game length barely changes (+0.2 to +1.1 min median). The storm is a small part of how games end here; attacks do most of the eliminating.
- 4 min cuts stalls at 4 and 6 players (3.7% → 1.3%, 8.3% → 1.3%); 3p is noise at 300 runs (0.3–2.0%).
- Storm deaths drop by ~70%, so the storm becomes pressure (a Dead card or two) rather than a killer. Attack eliminations rise to fill the gap.
- Cautious bots gain a few points of win rate as the storm weakens (3p 45% → 49%).
- Cubes: `--start-cubes` default is 0.5, i.e. half the tiles, which is what every run here used.

## Addendum 15 — Forced discard at end of turn (2026-10-13)

Designer asked: what if you must discard every non-Dead card at end of turn (normal deck-builder rule)? One variable: `--keep` (cards a bot holds over; Dead cards always stay). 300 runs, mixed bots, storm every 4 min, drops 2 min. Current rule is `--keep 3` (bots hold up to 3, Heals first).

| Players | Keep | Stalls | Median min (p10–p90) | Turns/game | Attacks/game | Buys/game | Cautious win % |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 3 (now) | 2.0% | 8.6 (5.6–13.0) | 28.8 | 19.3 | 9.3 | 49 |
| 3 | 1 | 0.0% | 7.7 (5.1–10.3) | 21.2 | 12.5 | 9.1 | 57 |
| 3 | 0 (forced) | 0.0% | 6.3 (4.1–9.1) | 17.1 | 10.2 | 8.1 | 58 |
| 4 | 3 (now) | 1.3% | 11.0 (7.0–15.6) | 36.6 | 25.3 | 12.2 | 41 |
| 4 | 1 | 0.0% | 9.0 (6.4–12.8) | 28.2 | 18.5 | 11.7 | 45 |
| 4 | 0 (forced) | 0.0% | 7.5 (5.3–10.2) | 23.6 | 15.3 | 10.4 | 42 |
| 6 | 3 (now) | 1.3% | 13.9 (9.9–19.0) | 54.7 | 36.3 | 17.4 | 43 |
| 6 | 1 | 0.0% | 12.0 (9.3–15.9) | 45.0 | 29.4 | 17.1 | 56 |
| 6 | 0 (forced) | 0.0% | 9.6 (7.1–13.1) | 36.8 | 23.6 | 15.4 | 58 |

- Forced discard makes games ~25% shorter (3p 8.6 → 6.3 min, 6p 13.9 → 9.6) and removes stalls. Eliminations per game are unchanged; there are just fewer wasted turns.
- Market buys fall 10–15%: unspent cards can't be saved toward a cost.
- Cautious bots win more at 3p and 6p (49 → 58%, 43 → 58%), not less. Likely bot artifact: forced discard punishes aggressive bots that build attack totals across turns; unverified with humans.
- Not modelled: bots don't change play when they know cards will be lost (e.g. spending everything before ending). A human forced-discard game would plan differently.
- Dead cards stay either way, so the hand-cap/3-Dead elimination pressure is unchanged.
