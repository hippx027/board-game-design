# SIM-002 — Designer fixes: no-island storm, push-off, pricier upgrades

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date | 2026-09-29 |
| Base rules | Playtest Rules v1 + variants below |
| Runner | `../sim/lds_sim.py` (lds-sim 0.1 + `--storm connected --push --upgrade-cost`) |
| Seed / runs | 42 / 300 per player count · mixed population · raw data `data2/*.json` |

## Variants
- **No islands:** the storm may only remove an edge tile whose removal leaves the board in one piece.
- **Push-off:** when active, the storm may remove a tile with a pawn on it. The pawn's owner moves it to an adjacent tile. Tested as "starts when no other tile can be removed" (auto), from round 8, and from round 12.
- **Upgrade payment:** payment cards go to the discard pile, and upgrades cost 4, 5 or 6.

## Result
**No-islands alone fixes almost all stalls (from 69% down to 2% at 5 players). Auto push-off clears the rest. An upgrade cost of 5 with payment to the discard pile keeps upgrade frequency close to v1.**

| Variant | Stall 5p / 10p | Median rounds 3/5/8/10p | Turns 5p / 10p | Upgrades per game, 5p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1 baseline (SIM-001) | 69% / 98% | 11 / 15 / 16 / 17 | — | 11.3 |
| No islands | 2% / 5% | 11 / 13 / 15 / 17 | 55 / 117 | 10.4 |
| + auto push-off | 1% / 0% | 11 / 13 / 16 / 17 | 53 / 104 | 10.4 |
| + push from round 8 | 0% / 0% | 11 / 13 / 15 / 16 | 51 / 104 | 10.3 |
| + auto push, discard, cost 4 | 0% / 0% | 12 / 15 / 16 / 17 | 55 / 105 | 14.7 |
| **+ auto push, discard, cost 5** | **0% / 0%** | **13 / 16 / 18 / 18** | **60 / 116** | **6.5** |
| + auto push, discard, cost 6 | 0% / 0% | 13 / 17 / 20 / 21 | 62 / 121 | 2.0 |

- Push-off from a fixed round pushes pawns often (7 per game at 5p from round 8). Auto push-off almost never fires (0.2–1 per game). It works as a safety valve.
- Cost 6 nearly kills upgrades, at about 1 per player per game. Cost 5 is the middle ground.
- With payments going to the discard pile, upgrades no longer thin decks, so the dead-card flood lands again.

## Still open
- **Turn-order advantage:** seat 1 wins 25–31% of 5-player games against a fair share of 20%. The last seat wins 11–14%. This held across every variant, so it's a separate problem.
- **Length at high player counts:** 10 players ≈ 105–120 turns. At about 1 minute per turn, that is well past 45 minutes. 5 players ≈ 55–60 turns fits.
- **Supply decks at 8+ players:** 12-card decks still run out; see SIM-001.
