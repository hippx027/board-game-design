# SIM-007 — Play every card, sticky Dead cards, end-of-turn elimination, merged Heal/Shield

Date 2026-09-29 · current rules otherwise (tiles 15/12/10 by player count) · 300 runs per cell · raw data `data6/`

Each variant adds one change on top of the one before:
- **A** Current rules (up to 2 plays, elimination at upkeep)
- **B** Play every card (one attack per turn still)
- **C** B + Dead cards stay in hand at end of turn + elimination checked at end of turn
- **D** C + Heal/Shield merged: each Heal card is spent either to remove that many Dead cards from hand (card leaves the game) or to gain that much Shield (card is discarded)
- **E** D, but a Heal card used to heal is also discarded (it never leaves the game)

## Findings
- **Playing every card fixes the turtling problem.** It's the first change in any test that closed the cautious edge (48% → 36% at 5p). All-in turns are now possible, so the player who commits can finish a fight before third parties arrive.
- **Sticky Dead cards + end-of-turn elimination speed the game up too much on their own** (9 rounds at 5p, barely reaching storm Phase 2).
- **Merging Heal/Shield (D) restores length** (13 rounds / 48 turns at 5p) and gives the most even spread between play styles. About 1 in 5 last-chance turns ends in a save.
- **Discarding Heals after healing (E)** swings too far: cautious play drops to 26% and games stretch back to 15 rounds.
- The upgrade "bundle as one play" rule is moot with unlimited plays. Upgrades drop to about 3 per 5-player game.
