## Design issues (for the designer)
1. **Shield turtle:** persistent Shield + holding Patch Ups tops Shield back to 4 every turn; early attacks rarely beat 4
2. **Base Shield 4:** upgraded Base Shield gives 4 free Shield every turn
3. **2p stalling:** players can avoid each other until the storm decides the game
4. **Storm marker placement is slow at 5p** (about 10 placements a round from round 7); a fixed ring order was suggested
5. **Nudging** mostly cancels out, or pulls the drop to the nudger

Checked and rejected: "the Loot Tile Set table totals 20 icons" was a miscount (it is 6/11/4 = 21).

## Wording fixes applied
Drop placement fallback; nudges may go anywhere; first drop if the start player is out · endgame line once every tile is storm · storm ignores and doesn't use Shield · "counts as played" moved to Cards; unused heal value is lost · draw-at-least-2 caveat when piles are empty · looting section split into its 3 options; cube rules separated; dead cubes stay on board · compact-board tip · character wording: Blaze/Shade timing and range; Ember = 1 extra cube from the same tile; Nova from hand; Gale (none in rounds 1–6); Vex after Shield; Bastion Base Shield still 0; Rig first upgrade of the game; starting stats of 2 can upgrade to 4
