No stalls in any variant. Balance between play styles barely moves. **One-time Heal cards (both modes) make games about 3 rounds / 11 turns shorter at 5p**: healing is scarcer, so the storm needs to do less of the killing. Recycling Heal cards (last row) stretches games to 17 rounds.
