## Findings
- **The flip storm works.** It never stalls, games stay at about 12–13 rounds, and players take about 2–3 storm Dead cards each per game at 5 players. The board never splits, so no-islands and push-off are no longer needed.
- **Reverse placement now hurts.** Once players can place near each other, the start player placing last lets them set up a round-1 strike. Going back to the normal placement order (start player places first) fixes 4–5 player seat balance (F). Wider spacing (≥5, G) also helps.
- **2–3 players still favour the first player** in every variant (2p: 60–66%; 3p: 39–45% against 50% / 33%). A catch-up for the second player is worth a separate test.
- ~~Keeping 1 card doesn't make critical turns survivable~~. That result came from a bot bug; see the addendum below.
- **No attacks in round 1** doesn't fix seat balance.
- The flip storm tilts slightly toward cautious play at 4–5 players (44–45%). Spacing ≥5 pulls it back (38%).

## Addendum: holding cards (bots fixed to always heal when it saves them)
Setup: flip storm, start ≥3 apart, normal placement order. 300 runs each. "Hold N" = keep up to N unplayed cards at the end of your turn (Dead cards stay regardless).

| Hold | 2p seats (fair 50/50) | 5p median rounds / turns | 5p seat range | 5p Aggressive / Balanced / Cautious | 5p Dead healed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | 57 / 43 | 12 / 44 | 17–23% | 19 / 38 / 43 | 12.6 |
| 1 | 54 / 46 | 14 / 51 | 17–22% | 12 / 36 / 52 | 16.4 |
| 2 | 56 / 44 | 14 / 52 | 15–25% | 11 / 35 / 55 | 16.0 |
| All | **48 / 52** | 15 / 54 | 16–24% | 13 / 35 / 52 | 15.6 |

Holding makes Heal cards a stockpile. Players heal more, games run about 2–3 rounds longer, and cautious play comes back (about 52% at 5p). Holding everything is the only rule that fixes the 2-player first-player edge.

Note: earlier SIM-008 "hold 1" results came from a bot bug (bots hoarded Heal cards instead of saving themselves) and are superseded by this table.
