# SIM-009 — Holding all cards + Fortnite-style storm phases

Date 2026-09-29 · base: rules v1 + storm markers (a marked tile deals Dead cards to anyone who ends a turn there) · start ≥3 apart · normal placement order · hold all cards (hand limit 5, Dead cards included) · 300 runs per cell · raw data `data8/`

## Storm schedules (5 players)
"Markers" = storm markers placed per turn; "Dmg" = Dead cards for ending a turn on a marked tile.

| Schedule | Median rounds | Turns | Storm Dead / game | Seats (fair 20%) | Aggressive / Balanced / Cautious |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| P0 flat: 1 marker, dmg 1 | 14.5 | 54 | 19 | 15–24% | 8 / 43 / 48 |
| P1 drop, then close: 0 markers R1–3, 1 from R4, 2 from R9; dmg 2 from R9 | 15 | 56 | 24 | 13–25% | 10 / 36 / 53 |
| **P2 close fast: 1 marker, 2 from R7; dmg 2 from R7** | **13** | **50** | 33 | **17–23%** | 9 / 39 / 52 |
| P3 waves: pauses between closes, dmg 1 → 2 → 3 | 15 | 55 | 37 | 16–23% | 9 / 38 / 53 |
| P4 drop + ramp: 0 → 1 → 2 → 3 markers, dmg 1 → 2 → 3 | 15 | 56 | 35 | 15–23% | 9 / 37 / 54 |

2 players with P2: seats 50 / 50 (the first-player edge is gone). No schedule stalls.

## Heal restrictions (with P2)
| Rule | 5p rounds / turns | 5p Aggressive / Balanced / Cautious |
|---|---|---|
| None | 13 / 50 | 9 / 39 / 52 |
| Healing means no attack that turn | 14 / 54 | 13 / 36 / 52 |
| Healing means no movement that turn | 14 / 52 | 11 / 38 / 51 |
| Both | 14 / 53 | 19 / 34 / 47 |
| **Heal cards can't be held (everything else can)** | **11 / 43** | **21 / 38 / 41** |

## Findings
- **The storm schedule doesn't change who wins.** Cautious play wins about 50% with holding, whatever the storm does. P2 (speed up at round 7) is the shortest and fairest schedule.
- **Holding Heal cards is what drives turtling.** Letting players hold everything except Heal cards cuts cautious wins from 52% to 41% and shortens games to about 11 rounds.
- Healing costs (no attack or no move when healing) barely help.
- Holding fixes the 2-player first-player edge under every schedule.
