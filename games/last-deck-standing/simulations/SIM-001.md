# SIM-001 — Rules v1 baseline: does the game end, and how long does it take?

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Date | 2026-09-29 |
| Rules version | Playtest Rules v1 (`../rulebook-draft.md`) |
| Runner | project-local `../sim/lds_sim.py` (lds-sim 0.1) |
| Seed / runs | 42 / 500 per player count |
| Players | 3, 5, 8, 10 |
| Population | mixed (aggressive / balanced / cautious heuristic bots) |
| Fidelity | P1 simulation. System evidence only; says nothing about fun or clarity |
| Raw data | `data/*.json` |

## Result
**Rules v1 often never ends.** The storm strips every tile except the ones players stand on. Survivors are left on one-tile islands, out of each other's range and unable to move. Upgrades also thin decks to 1–7 cards, so a deck rarely holds the 3 Dead cards needed to eliminate its owner.

| Players | Games that stall | Median rounds (finished games) | Seat 1 win rate (fair share) | Last seat win rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3 | 31% | 11 | 38% (33%) | 24% |
| 5 | 69% | 15 | 21% (20%) | 12% |
| 8 | 93% | 16 | 11% (12.5%) | 11% |
| 10 | 98% | 17 | 8% (10%) | 8% |

A game counts as stalled at 150 rounds without a winner. Attacks land on only about 5% of turns.

## Candidate fixes (one variable each; not rule changes)
Stall rate by player count:

| Variant | 3p | 5p | 8p | 10p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| v1 baseline | 31% | 69% | 93% | 98% |
| Board fixed at 70 tiles | 23% | 70% | 91% | 97% |
| Storm is a closing circle: always remove the edge tile farthest from the board's centre | 7% | 32% | 70% | 82% |
| Upgrade payment goes to discard, not out of the game | 7% | 28% | 58% | 74% |
| Both of the two above | 1% | 8% | 30% | 42% |

- Board size alone does nothing. The stall is structural.
- At 8+ players, all three 12-card supply decks run out in over 90% of games in every variant.
- The last seat wins about 40% less often than a fair share at 3–5 players. This deserves its own test once games reliably end.

## Caveats
- The bots are heuristic and cautious about danger. Humans may attack more, which would shorten games.
- Randomly shaped boards: real boards built by players may be rounder.

## Next
Hypothesis: a storm that converges players toward each other, plus a guaranteed endgame pressure, drops stalls below 5% at 5 players. Candidate endgame pressures: storm damage or a forced-off rule, both already in Ideas for Later.
