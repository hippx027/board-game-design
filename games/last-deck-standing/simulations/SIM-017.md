# SIM-017 — Heal card: heal OR shield vs heal AND shield

Date 2026-09-29 · current rules · 400 runs per cell · raw data `data15/`
Combo = a Heal card heals up to its value **and** gives that much Shield; it leaves the game only if it healed something.

| Mode | 5p rounds / turns | 5p Dead healed | 5p damage absorbed by Shield | 5p Aggressive / Balanced / Cautious | 5p seat range |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Heal OR Shield (current)** | 14 / 53 | 13.1 | 20% | 10 / 36 / 55 | 16–23% |
| Heal AND Shield | 14 / 55 | 14.4 | 25% | 13 / 37 / 50 | 16–21% |

Combo makes every Heal card stronger: Shield blocks a quarter of all damage and games run about 2 turns longer, but balance barely moves. It also removes the heal-or-shield decision. **Kept: heal OR shield.**
