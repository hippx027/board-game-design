# SIM-018 — Characters: base stats + one ability

Date 2026-09-29 · current rules · random characters per seat, mixed bot styles · 900–1500 runs per cell · scripts `char_test.py`, `char_tune.py`
Win rate per character when in the game (fair: 2p 50%, 3p 33%, 4p 25%, 5p 20%).

Abilities: **Point blank** +1 damage at range 0 · **Scavenge** loot twice per turn · **Storm runner** storm damage −1 · **Field medic** each Heal card used to heal removes 1 extra Dead card · **Long shot** +1 damage at range ≥ N.

Patterns: **+1 Move is the strongest stat at 4–5 players** (more reach, more loot, dodging the storm). **+1 Shield on Nova tips her over.** Attack bonuses (Blaze, Shade) are strong in duels and weak in crowds, the same third-party effect as before. Bots use abilities greedily, not cleverly; treat this as a starting point for human tests.
