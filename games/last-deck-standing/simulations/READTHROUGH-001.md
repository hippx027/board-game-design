# READTHROUGH-001 — Blind rulebook read-throughs (2p, 4p, 5p)

Date 2026-09-29 · three Claude (Opus) agents, each given only the main rules (no Designer Notes), with scripted play and real shuffles (seeds 101 / 202 / 303). Logs are in the session scratchpad.

| Game | Winner | Length | How it went |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2p | P1 | Round 3 (6 turns) | Both pawns on one hex, trading 3 Dead cards a turn; the first reshuffle eliminated the other player |
| 4p | Upgrader | Round 16 | Aggro dealt 39 Dead cards but lost to the player who stayed out of range |
| 5p | B | Round 9 (39 turns) | The others paired off at range 0; B stayed apart, looted a 5-card pile and won |

## Design decisions needed
1. **Range-0 stacking:** pawns on the same hex trade maximum damage every turn (all 3 games).
2. **Bundled plays are strong:** after one Attack upgrade, a player played every Attack card as one play and hit for 5 every turn (4p).
3. **Dead cards are hidden:** they sit in the discard pile, so a reshuffle eliminates players with no warning (all 3 games).
4. **Upgrades barely happen in short games:** nobody upgraded in the 5p game.
5. **Setup is heavy:** 75 tiles and about 225 dice rolls at 5 players.
6. **Dead supply:** 30 is too few; 62 were dealt in the 5p game.

## Wording fixes (no design change)
- The Heal deck's exact Heal/Shield split per value
- Heal and Shield count as one type for upgrades and bundled plays
- Played Shield cards go to the discard pile; only played Heal cards leave the game
- Heal only affects Dead cards in your hand
- Shield points are spent as they absorb damage
- The active player chooses the storm tile; the pawn's owner chooses the push-off tile
- A loot action is either 1 cube or 1 loot-pile pick, never both; an empty pile is removed
- The round marker advances when play passes the start player's seat, even after the start player is eliminated
- The storm never removes the last tile
- Component quantities: loot cubes (about 40 per colour), round track (25+ spaces), stat track (1–4)
- Range is straight-line hex distance, and adjacent hexes are range 1
