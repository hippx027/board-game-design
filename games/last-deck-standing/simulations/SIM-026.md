# SIM-026 — No Shield refill (Heal bonus upgrade) and split attacks

Date 2026-09-30 · current rules · 500 runs per player count · script `shield_attack_test.py`

| Variant | 5p rounds / turns | 5p Shield share of blocked damage | 5p Dead healed | 5p Aggressive / Balanced / Cautious | 2p seats |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Current (upkeep refills to Base Shield) | 14 / 51 | 20% | 12.9 | 12 / 39 / 49 | 56 / 44 |
| No refill; Heal upgrade = +1 to every Heal card (max +3) | 14 / 52 | 17% | 13.9 | 11 / 41 / 47 | 58 / 42 |
| Split attacks | 14 / 51 | 20% | 12.9 | 12 / 39 / 49 | 56 / 44 |
| **Both (adopted)** | 14 / 51 | 17% | 13.9 | 11 / 41 / 48 | 58 / 42 |

No stalls at any player count. No refill removes the free-Shield-every-turn exploit (READTHROUGH-004/005) and shifts value from Shield to healing. Bots rarely split attacks (splitting pays range again), so split attacks are a tactical option for humans with no measurable balance cost. The Tank becomes "start with 2 Shield points, upgrades cost 5" (not yet re-tested).
