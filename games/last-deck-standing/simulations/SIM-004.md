# SIM-004 — Supply size, gold drops, and why cautious play wins

Date 2026-09-29 · current rules (no-island storm, push-off from round 8, reverse placement, upgrade cost 5 to discard) · 300 runs per cell · raw data `data5/*.json`

## Supply size (5 players)
| Supply decks | Games where a deck runs out |
|---|---|
| 12 cards | 78% |
| 18 cards | 25% |
| 18 cards + gold drops at rounds 4/8/12 | 15% |
| 24 cards | 3% |

Game length is unchanged (median 16–17 rounds). **Adopted: 18 cards.**

## Cautious play
Win rate of one bot style among 4 random opponents (fair share 20%):

| Change | Aggressive | Balanced | Cautious |
|---|---|---|---|
| None | 11% | 18% | 25% |
| Eliminator gets 3 of the eliminated player's cards | 11% | 20% | 23% |
| Eliminator gets +1 stat | 10% | 17% | 27% |
| Both | 13% | 20% | 21% |

- Trait tests: danger avoidance is the cause. Shield upgrades have no effect. Letting bots retreat after attacking has no effect.
- Elimination rewards barely help, because the last hit often comes from a bystander who picks off a weakened player.
- Next candidates: a reward on every hit that lands, or pressure on players who hang back.
- Caveat: heuristic bots. Confirm with human playtests.

## Addendum: elimination loot pile
The eliminated player's cards stay on their hex as a pile. Looting it draws N cards, with Dead cards redrawn. 5 players, 200 runs, gold drops on. Win rates:

| Variant | Aggressive | Balanced | Cautious | Median rounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Kill cache (current) | 14% | 23% | 29% | 17 |
| Pile of 5, no cost | 12% | 20% | 26% | 18 |
| Pile of 5, costs 2 movement | 11% | 24% | 27% | 18 |
| Pile of 3, costs 2 movement | 9% | 21% | 26% | 18 |

No effect on the cautious edge (differences are within about ±3% run-to-run noise). The piles get looted by whoever is nearby, and cautious bots take them too.
