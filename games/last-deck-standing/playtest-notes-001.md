# Playtest 001 — Designer Notes (verbatim)

Recorded 2026-10-12 from the first human session. These are the designers' takeaways, not yet rules. `rulebook-draft.md` is unchanged.

- Switching to realtime
    - We need to work out how attacks do that, maybe a pause like Captain Sonar
    - We think you put Dead cards in a player's deck yourself
- Base deck 4 move, 4 fight, 2 heal.
- Upgrade cards are 2+, never 1s.
- No more rounds
    - Loot drops every 2ish min.
    - Storm needs to be on a timer as well
- Market of cards to purchase, with combos move, heal, attack costs. With increasing tiers.
- Remove stats; it's card based only. You are trying to upgrade your deck instead of your stats
- Cubes for loot, recalibrate.
    - No more must-tile loot; you get a loot for each tile you move through (play test this)
- Real time teams
    - Each team member takes their turn rotating
        - So member 1 goes and does a full turn, then member 2 does, but it's realtime against the other team

Paper size update
- 11x17 paper for cards.
