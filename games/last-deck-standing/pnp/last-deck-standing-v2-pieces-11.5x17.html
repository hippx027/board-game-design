How to buy
  • Any time on your turn, pay the cards below from your hand. Costs count cards, not value, so pay with 1s.
  • Paid cards leave the game. The bought card goes to your discard pile.
  • Refill the face-up row from the deck after each buy.

QUICK REFERENCE

Your turn

  • Draw up to 5 cards. Say critical if 3+ Dead are in hand.
  • Play cards, move, loot, buy, attack, in any order.
  • End: 3+ Dead in hand = out. Otherwise discard what you don't want (never Dead) and go again. No turn timer.

Move and loot

  • Base Move 1 + Move cards played. One adjacent tile per point.
  • Moving into a tile with a cube takes it: draw the top card of that loot deck into your hand. Hand cap 5; extra loot goes to your discard pile.
  • Gold cubes on your own tile can be taken without moving.

Attack

  • Say Attack; the table pauses. Damage = Attack total − 2 per tile of range (Base Attack 1 + cards).
  • Put that many Dead cards in the target's discard pile. Then resume.

Timers

  • Storm (every 4 min): pause, mark the whole outer ring. Moving into a storm tile = 1 Dead; ending your turn on one = 1 more.
  • Drop (every 2 min): first player (rotates) places it, next moves it up to 3 tiles, table puts one gold cube per player on it.
